# Active plans

The [master continuation plan](master-continuation-plan.md) is the single
closure checklist for the first official release, `0.1.0`. It owns manual
gates, current dispositions, review-fix status and validation evidence.

M1–M6, the HR fit setting and the post-M6 stable-release corrections are merged
through [PR #139](https://github.com/SkynetRTN/MARS/pull/139), at `8a91523`
(2026-11-14). The implementation checklist is complete. Record the final
merged/tagged revision, index verification and publication outcome before
closing the remaining release-evidence gate.

Historical recipes are in [archived task plans](../archive/plans/README.md),
and broader tracks in [the archive](../archive/README.md). Original findings
and measurements stay in [dated reviews](../reviews/README.md). Unchecked boxes
in archived recipes retain original wording and do not reopen completed work.

When a work stream lands, update its current guide or architecture reference,
record its disposition in the master, and archive the completed plan with a
dated completion annotation. Archive the master only after accepted work is
closed and remaining scope has explicit deferral/revisit decisions. Preserve
the evidence and original finding IDs.
